Tableau Projects

The Ecommerce Sales Dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of sales performance. Here are the key highlights:
-SALES AND PROFITS:- Total sales are $14.11M, up by 10.72% from the previous year, while profit is $1.30M, down by 0.46%.
-ORDER QUANTITY:- 157,978 orders, which is a 15.02% decrease.
-MARKET SHARE:- North America leads with $6.13M in sales.
-CATEGORY SALES:- Detailed comparison of year-to-date (YTD) sales versus previous year-to-date (PYTD) sales for various categories like Camping Equipment and Golf Accessories.
-CUSTOMER ACQUISITION:- Monthly trends in customer acquisition across different markets.
SALES TRENDS:- Monthly sales data across various regions, showing trends over time.
RECOMMENDATIONS
1.Focus on Profit Improvement: Despite high sales of $14.11M, the profit margin is relatively low at $1.30M with a slight decrease of 0.46%. Investigate cost-saving measures or pricing strategies to improve profitability.
2.Address Declining Order Quantity: The order quantity has decreased by 15.02%. Analyze customer feedback and market trends to understand the reasons behind this decline and implement strategies to boost order volumes.
3.Leverage High-Performing Markets: North America has the largest market share by sales value. Consider investing more in marketing and customer acquisition efforts in this region to capitalize on its strong performance.

HR Analytics Dashboard provides a detailed overview of employee-related metrics within a company. Here are the key highlights:
-EMPLOYEE COUNT:- Total number of employees is 1,470.
-ATTRITION RATE:- The attrition rate is 16.12%, with 237 employees leaving.
-DEPARTMENT-WISE DISTRIBUTION:- A pie chart shows the distribution of employees across different departments.
-AGE GROUP DISTRIBUTION:- A bar graph depicts the number of employees in various age groups.
-JOB SATISFACTION:- Tables provide job satisfaction ratings and attrition details by gender.
RECOMMENDATIONS
1. Address High Attrition: With an attrition rate of 16.12%, it's crucial to investigate the reasons behind employee turnover, especially in departments with higher rates. Conduct exit interviews and employee surveys to gather insights.
2. Targeted Retention Strategies: Develop retention strategies tailored to different age groups and genders, as the attrition rate varies across these demographics. Personalized approaches can help in addressing specific needs and concerns
